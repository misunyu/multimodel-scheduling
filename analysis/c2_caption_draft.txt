Primary (concise):
"Per-size decomposition of the two loss mechanisms (YOLOv11s, single stream): INT8
quantization loss Q_b (4-thread NPU minus FP32 GPU) and delivery staleness loss L_b
(24- minus 4-thread NPU). Quantization falls almost entirely on small/medium objects
and is negligible on large objects (+0.001), whereas staleness is negligible on small
objects and largest on large objects (-0.098) — the opposite size dependence that drives
the placement reversal."

Alternative (adds error-bar note):
"Absolute sAP loss per size bin for quantization (Q_b) and staleness (L_b); error bars are
the standard deviation over three runs. The two mechanisms have opposite size dependence:
Q_large is approximately zero while L_large is the largest single-bin loss, so the large-object
bin is where All-NPU overtakes All-GPU under contention."

Numbers in figure (abs ΔsAP):
  Q: small -0.008, medium -0.036, large +0.001
  L: small -0.001, medium -0.029(-0.030 rounded), large -0.098
Relative %% vs FP32 GPU baseline: Q -48.4/-19.7/+0.1 ; L -8.0/-16.1/-20.5
