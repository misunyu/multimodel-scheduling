### How `q_share = 169%` arises

`q_share = (quantization Δ) / (total NPU-path Δ) = +0.0570 / +0.0338 = 169%`.

The share exceeds 100% because two of the offset components have the OPPOSITE sign of the quantization drop:

- **Background-on-NPU** is **positive** (+0.0256): NPU at L1_light (0.0835) actually scored HIGHER than NPU at L0 (0.0579) for this sid. The single-stream NPU 'penalty from background' is negative — the worst-camera sid recovers under L1_light single-stream.
- **Concurrent-NPU** is **positive** (+0.0001): worst sAP for the worst sid under AllNPU N=8 L0 was HIGHER than NPU N=1 L1. Multi-stream AllNPU did not damage the worst sid relative to N=1.

The quantization delta therefore overshoots the total NPU-path loss; the two negative components push the total back down. This is a sid-specific arithmetic effect, not a measurement error.
